Pokémon Shiny Tracker

A clone of shinytrack.night.coffee by Tim Gourley, extended with every Pokémon through Generation 9 (Scarlet/Violet and its DLC) and Gen 9 shiny mechanics.

Pokémon and all Pokémon data is owned by The Pokémon Company, used without permission. Sprites are served by Pokémon Showdown.

FAQ

  • How do I use this?
    On the Setup tab pick your game's generation, whether you have the Shiny Charm, and your hunting method. In Gen 9 you can also set your sandwich's Sparkling Power level and mass-outbreak progress. Optionally type a target Pokémon for motivation, then head to Track and count encounters. Use + Add hunt to hunt several Pokémon at once; each has its own count and settings, and Setup edits the one selected on Track.
  • Shortcuts?
    On Track, +, Space or ↑ adds an encounter to the selected hunt; − or ↓ takes one away. Click a hunt (not its buttons) to show just that hunt, larger; click it again or press Esc to see all hunts.
  • Gen 9 odds?
    Base 1/4096. Each bonus adds extra rolls: Shiny Charm +2, Sparkling Power Lv 1/2/3 adds 1/2/3, mass outbreaks add 1 after 30 KOs and 2 after 60. Everything stacked is 1/512. Masuda Method eggs are 1/683, or 1/512 with the charm; sandwiches don't affect eggs.
  • What does B(n,p) mean?
    The binomial distribution: the chance you would already have found at least one shiny in the encounters you've done so far.
  • And "Until 90%"?
    How many more encounters until that chance hits 90%. If you haven't found one by then, you might consider taking a break.

All numbers are probabilities, not guarantees. Your settings and count are stored in this browser (localStorage). If you sign in under Setup → Sync, they're also saved to this site's server so your other devices can load them.
